How to Adopt

Follow these simple steps to bring your new best friend home:

  1. Find a Match: Browse our "Our Pets" page to find a dog or cat that fits your lifestyle.
  2. Submit an Application: Let us know a little about your home and experience with pets.
  3. Meet & Greet: Schedule a time to visit the shelter and spend time with your chosen pet.
  4. Finalize the Adoption: Sign the paperwork, pay the adoption fee, and take your pet home!
